Tianji for Android — Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 23, 2026

Zhan Rushman LLC ("Zhan Rushman," "we," "us," or "our") publishes Tianji for Android (the "App") on Google Play. This Privacy Policy explains what information the App handles, where it goes, and the choices you have. The iPhone version of Tianji has its own policy: Privacy Policy (iPhone).

In short. Your chart is calculated on your phone and stays there, with your birth details, your journal and your readings. There is no Tianji account and no Tianji server. Only two things ever leave the phone, and only when you start them: the place name you type when you search for your birthplace, and a purchase through Google Play. The App contains no AI service, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking.

1. INFORMATION THE APP HANDLES

Your birth details

To calculate your chart, the App asks for your date of birth, your time of birth (or that you are not sure of it), your place of birth, and the direction your chart is counted in (which the App asks as a sex, for the chart only). These are stored on your device and are never sent to us or to anyone else.

Your place-of-birth search

To find your place of birth, the words you type are looked up by your phone's place-search service (Android's geocoder; on most Android phones this is provided by Google), which returns matching places. That provider handles the search under its own privacy policy (for Google: policies.google.com/privacy). Only the place you pick is kept, and it is kept on your device. Your birth date and time are never sent with the search. The App does not use your location for this.

Your journal and readings

The notes, moods and outcomes you write in the journal and the readings you keep are stored on your device. None of this is sent anywhere.

Readings

Every reading in the App comes from the reviewed texts that ship with it. Nothing is sent to an AI service. If a later version offers readings written by one, the App will ask you first, and this Policy will be updated before that version is released.

Motion

The App uses your phone's motion sensor to feel you shake the bamboo cylinder when you draw a stick. This is used on your device at that moment and is never stored or sent. The App does not use your location, microphone, camera or contacts.

Purchases

The Plus subscription is sold and processed entirely by Google Play. We receive confirmation that a purchase was made and its status, but never your payment details or your Google account details.

Usage statistics, advertising and tracking

The App contains no analytics, advertising or tracking code, does not use the Android advertising ID, and sends no usage statistics. If a later version adds usage statistics, it will ask you first where the law requires it, and this Policy will be updated before that version is released.

2. HOW WE USE INFORMATION

The information above is used only to provide the App's features on your device: to calculate your chart, show your readings and keep your journal. We do not receive it, and we do not use it for advertising or profiling.

3. SHARING OF INFORMATION

We do not sell, rent or share your information. The only parties the App involves are your phone's place-search provider, for the words you type into the birthplace search, and Google Play, for purchases, each under its own privacy policy.

4. DATA RETENTION AND YOUR CHOICES

Everything the App stores stays on your device until you delete it. You can:

  • Delete everything under Settings → Your data → Delete everything, which removes your chart, journal, day count, saved readings and everything cached from them.
  • Export your data first, under Settings → Your data → Export my data, as a file you choose where to save.
  • Uninstall the App, which removes everything it stored on the device.

5. YOUR PRIVACY RIGHTS

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct, delete, or receive a copy of your personal information, and to object to or restrict certain processing. Because your data is stored on your own device rather than on our servers, you can exercise these rights directly in the App (see Section 4). For anything else, contact us at zhanrushmanllc@gmail.com and we'll respond within a reasonable time.

California residents: we do not sell or share personal information as defined by the California Consumer Privacy Act.

European/UK residents: where the GDPR or UK GDPR applies, the birthplace search is carried out because you ask for it, to provide the feature you are using, and purchases are processed by Google Play to perform the contract you enter into.

6. CHILDREN'S PRIVACY

Tianji is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, contact us at zhanrushmanllc@gmail.com and we will take appropriate steps to remove it.

7. SECURITY

We rely on Android's platform security (app-private storage and device encryption) to protect data stored on your device, and on encrypted connections (HTTPS/TLS) for the place search and Google Play. No method of transmission or storage is 100% secure, and we can't guarantee absolute security.

8. CHANGES TO THIS POLICY

We may update this Privacy Policy as the App evolves. If we make material changes, we'll update the effective date above and, where appropriate, tell you in the App. A change that would send something new from your device is asked about in the App before it happens.
