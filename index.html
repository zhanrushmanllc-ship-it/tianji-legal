FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

What is Tianji?

Tianji draws a chart from the moment you were born, using the Four Pillars and Zi Wei Dou Shu. Each day it gives you one line; when something is on your mind, you can draw a stick or cast a hexagram, and keep what it said in your journal.

I don't know my birth time.

Choose “I'm not sure” on the hour step. The chart is drawn for noon and marked approximate. You can add the time later under Settings → Edit birth details.

What does “Written for you” mean?

Those passages are written by a third-party AI service from your derived chart — never your birth date, time or place. The app asks before anything is sent; you can change your answer under Settings → Your data. Without it, the readings keep the reviewed texts that come with the app.

Can I speak my question?

Yes: hold the Immortal on the Ask tab and speak. Recognition runs on your iPhone only and no recording is kept. The app explains this before iOS asks for the microphone and speech recognition; if you declined, enable them in iOS Settings → Tianji, or use the keyboard.

What does Plus include?

Plus is an auto-renewing subscription (monthly, or yearly with a free trial) that opens the full daily reading, every casting method, the annual report, the feng shui compass and the follow-up conversation. Without Plus, a year’s annual report can also be bought on its own.

How do I restore a purchase on a new device?

Settings → Restore purchases. Make sure you are signed in with the same Apple ID that made the original purchase.

How do I cancel my subscription?

Settings → Manage subscription, which opens Apple's subscription screen, or iOS Settings → your name → Subscriptions. Cancelling stops the next renewal; you keep Plus until the current period ends. Refunds are handled by Apple.

Where is my data stored?

On your iPhone. There is no Tianji account and no Tianji server. What leaves your iPhone, and when, is set out in the Privacy Policy.

How do I delete my data?

Settings → Your data → Delete everything removes your chart, journal and saved readings, and resets every consent. You can export your data first from the same screen.

Can I turn off usage statistics?

Yes, under Settings → Your data → Usage statistics, wherever you are. In the EEA, the UK and Switzerland they are off until you allow them.

Which languages are supported?

English and Traditional Chinese (繁體中文), which you can choose under Settings → Language.

What are the system requirements?

An iPhone running iOS 17.0 or later.

Is this a prediction or advice?

No. Tianji presents what a tradition says, for reflection and entertainment. It is not a prediction, and not medical, psychological, financial or other professional advice.
